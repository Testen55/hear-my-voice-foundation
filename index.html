Hear My Voice Foundation

Supporting single fathers and their children — because no father raising his kids on his own should have to do it without a community behind him.

Our Mission

Hear My Voice Foundation exists to give single fathers a voice — and to surround them and their children with encouragement, community connection, and practical support. Fathers matter in a child's life, and a father carrying his family alone deserves to be heard, respected, and backed up.

Who We Are

Hear My Voice Foundation was established on August 8, 2010. From our earliest days we have brought families together — including a Family Fall Festival for parents and children at High Park in Toronto in October 2010 — around one simple belief: strong fathers build strong children, and strong children build strong communities.

A foundation for single fathers, run by people who know the road.
