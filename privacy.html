Privacy Policy

Hear My Voice Foundation — effective November 24, 2026

Hear My Voice Foundation ("the Foundation," "we," "us") is a community foundation established in 2010 to support single fathers and their children. This policy explains, in plain language, what information we collect and what we do with it.

Our website

Our website is a simple information page. It has no accounts, no forms, no cookies set by us, and no advertising or analytics trackers. Our website host may keep standard server logs (such as IP addresses and pages visited) to operate and secure the site, as every web host does.

When you contact us

If you email us, we receive your name, your email address, and whatever you write in your message. We use that information only to reply to you and to carry on the Foundation activity you contacted us about. We do not sell personal information, and we do not share it with anyone else except where the law requires it.

Our connector and AI tools

The Foundation is setting up a connector so an AI assistant can help with Foundation activities. If you use it, the information you give the assistant is also handled under that AI provider's own privacy policy. Please do not share sensitive personal information — such as financial details, identification numbers, or health information — through the connector or by email.

Keeping and deleting information

We keep emails and records only as long as the Foundation needs them for its activities and legal obligations. If you want us to delete personal information you sent us, email us and we will do so unless the law requires us to keep it.
